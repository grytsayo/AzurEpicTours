Your journey

Your six days

01

Welcome to the Riviera

Meet us at Nice airport, transfer to your hotel and settle in. Enjoy a first walk by the sea and time to rest after your journey.

02

Discover Nice

Colourful façades, the old town and local flavours. In the evening, enjoy free time for a stroll or dinner.

03

Èze and Monaco

Èze above the sea and La Turbie, the Prince’s Palace and Monaco’s old town. Discover Monte Carlo and stop for coffee at Café de Paris.

04

Wine and art

Visit Château de Crémat for a wine tasting, discover the artists’ village of Saint-Paul-de-Vence and walk along the ramparts of Antibes.

05

Villas, gardens and sea

Villa Kérylos, a coastal walk around Cap-Ferrat and Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild. The afternoon is yours to relax by the sea.

06

Until next time

One last French breakfast and a transfer to Nice airport. We will arrange departure around your flight.
